      About the data

      Rain. DADOS DA REDE OBSERVACIONAL DO CEMADEN/MCTIC (CEMADEN observational network data), state Civil Defense rain gauges and National Water Agency (ANA) stations, most of them at dams and power plants. A municipality's value is the highest accumulation among its active stations. The 1 h, 6 h and 24 h buttons show what has already fallen; +6 h and +24 h show the forecast.

      Estimate and forecast. The forecast always comes from the model. Where there is no recent measurement, the map shows (translucent, with “~”) the rainfall estimated by the Open-Meteo model (CC BY 4.0) at the municipality centre. It is a model, not a rain gauge: it can be wrong, especially in localised storms.

      Rivers. Rio Grande do Sul Civil Defense hydrometeorological network. The glowing outline shows the status it reports (attention, alert or flood) and may reflect the highest recent level, not only the current one. ANA (HidroWebService) stations add the hourly gauge reading (stage), but ANA does not publish alert thresholds: they have no risk classification and are not part of the glowing outline or the river summary.

      News. Headlines and links from G1 RS and Agência Brasil (public RSS feeds). Headlines are machine-translated from Portuguese and picked automatically by keyword, which can be wrong. The content belongs to the outlets: the links open the original article.

      Susceptibility. Susceptibility charts for gravitational mass movements and floods from the Geological Survey of Brazil (SGB/CPRM), shown with source credit as advised by the SGB Ombudsman. They show where the terrain tends to flood or slide, at their own scale and for their own purpose (land-use planning): they do not show what is happening now nor today's rain. They exist only for some municipalities; where there is no chart, that does not mean there is no risk. The charts are always visible at any zoom; for mass movement only the medium and high classes are hatched, because the low class covers almost the whole municipality. The original SGB colours were replaced so they are not confused with rainfall.

      Map. IBGE municipal boundaries. Terrain: Mapzen/AWS Terrain Tiles.

      A dark grey municipality has no measurement and no estimate at the moment, which does not mean there is no rain.
